## Chemistry
Resources you will find helpful when studying for Chemistry Classes. 
1. [Chemistry Resource](https://chemistry-teaching-resources.com/): Videos with information on topics you would find in Chemistry of varying levels.
2. [Pearson Chemistry Study Guide](https://www.pearson.com/channels/general-chemistry/study-guides/comprehensive-study-notes-general-chemistry-topics-overview): Study guide for a variety of chemistry topics with written notes, flashcards, and structured videos.
3. [High School Chemistry Lecture - Chem 1- Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/hs-chemistry): This is for high school chemistry, however it could still prove useful for chemistry 1.
4. [Chemistry Notes Lecture](https://www.chemistrynoteslecture.com/): This provides resources for chemistry 1 along with video notes. 
5. [The Organic Chemistry Tutor](https://www.youtube.com/@TheOrganicChemistryTutor): A Youtube channel focused primarily on organic chemistry with courses on other topics like general chemistry or math.

## Physics
Resources you will find helpful when studying for Physics.
1. [Jake's Physics Notes](https://jakesphysicsnotes.com/): Primarily geared towards high school physics, this is still useful for physics 1.
2. [Pearson Physics Course](https://www.pearson.com/channels/physics/learn/patrick/math-review/math-review): Provides video guides through physics concepts covered primarily in physics 1. Also has practice sets and worksheets.
3. [Khan Academy - Physics 1](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-college-physics-1): Khan Academy course covering concepts related to physics 1.
4. [Khan Academy - Physics 2](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/ap-physics-2): Khan Academy course covering concepts related to physics 2, thermodynamics is included in this and may be in physics 1 courses at UC.
