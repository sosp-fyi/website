## Algebra

Resources that you will find helpful when studying for your algebra classes.

1. [Linear Algebra - Math - Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/linear-algebra): Videos with information on topics you would find in Linear Algebra.


## Calculus

Resources that you will find helpful when studying for your calculus classes.

1. [Pre-Calculus - Math - Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/precalculus): Videos with information on topics that you would find in Pre-Calculus.
2. [Calculus 1 - Math - Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/calculus-1): Videos with information on topics you would find in Calculus 1.
3. [Calculus 2 - Math - Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/math/calculus-2): Videos with infomration on topics you would find in Calculus 2.


## Discrete

Resources that you will find helpful when studying for your discrete math classes.

1. [Disctrete Structures - Math - Open Math Books](https://discrete.openmathbooks.org/dmoi4.html): A website that gives you a free electronic version of the Discrete Mathematics book by Oscar Levin.


## Statistics

Resources that you will find helpful when studying for your statistics classes.

1. [Statistics - Math - YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9FtHB7V14Fo&list=PL5102DFDC6790F3D0): A playlist of many full-length Statistics lecture videos, broken down by section.
