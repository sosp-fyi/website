## CS 1100 - Intro To Computer Science

Resources and Topics for CS 1110:

1. [Jobs in computer science](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/computer-and-information-technology/):
    - List of some jobs in computer science to help with week 1 and 2 assignment.
2. [Skills needed for jobs](https://www.nu.edu/blog/computer-science-skills/):
    - List of skills needed for your future career. Helps with class assignment #3 aswell!
3. [Selection and Insertion Sort](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/dsa/difference-between-insertion-sort-and-selection-sort/)
    - This will help you figure out what selection sort and insertion sort are, how they differ, and when each is better than the other! 
4. [DFA (Deterministic Finite Automata)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=19ygGQqolGI)
    - Video on DFA.
